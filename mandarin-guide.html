Speak Mandarin at work in 15 days

Author: Harsh Sharma

One workbook for an English and Hindi speaker who needs Chinese in meetings, stand-ups and code discussions. Tap 🔊 or any Chinese line to hear it. Pinyin is colored by tone everywhere, so your eyes learn the melody with the words.

Tap each one. Same syllable ma, four different words: mother, hemp, horse, scold. That difference is the whole game. Color code: blue sky (high), green sprout (rising), brown soil (low), red stop (falling).

Straight talk on 15 days. Nobody reaches full native-speed fluency in 15 days. What this plan does deliver, if you put in 3 to 4 focused hours a day: correct sounds and tones from the start (the part that is almost impossible to fix later), around 400 high-use words and chunks, a confident self-introduction, status updates, asking people to repeat or slow down, explaining a bug or a design choice in simple sentences, polite agreement and disagreement, and following clear, slower meeting talk on topics you know. Complex, fast, multi-person meetings take months of daily use. Day 16 onward is in the plan so it keeps compounding.

Your advantage as a Hindi speaker

Aspiration is already in your mouth

Chinese separates b/p, d/t, g/k, j/q, zh/ch, z/c by a puff of air, exactly like Hindi प/फ, त/थ, क/ख, च/छ. English speakers struggle with this. You don't.

Retroflex sounds

Chinese zh ch sh use a curled tongue, close to Hindi ट and ष. You already have the muscle.

Questions don't move

Hindi: आप क्या कर रहे हैं? Chinese: 你在做什么? The question word sits where the answer goes, same as Hindi.

Postpositions and comparisons

सर्वर पर = 服务器上. मीटिंग से पहले = 会议以前. A B से तेज़ = A 比 B 快. Same skeleton.

The 4 tones 声调

A tone is the pitch shape of a syllable. Picture your voice range as 5 levels, 5 is your comfortable top, 1 your comfortable bottom. Most Indian English is spoken in a narrow middle band; Mandarin needs the full range.

The biggest secret: the 3rd tone is a LOW tone. Textbooks draw it as a dip-and-rise (214). In real speech it is only fully dipped when alone or at the very end of a phrase, and even then lightly. Everywhere else, just drop to the bottom of your voice and stay there, like a low creaky हम्म. Learners who dip every 3rd tone sound slow and sing-song.

Hand gestures: while speaking, draw the tone in the air. Flat line high for 1, sweep up for 2, push down low for 3, chop down for 4. The hand drags the voice with it. Do this for the first week.

Hum first: for any new phrase, hum only the melody (mmm-mmm) 3 times, then add the words.

Questions keep their tones: in Hindi and English your pitch rises for questions. In Chinese the word 吗 or the question word does that job. Do not lift the last syllable, or you change its tone.

Consonants 声母

21 starting consonants plus y and w. The Hindi column gives the nearest sound you already own. Read the trap column twice.

Palm test for aspiration: hold your palm 3 cm from your lips. p t k q ch c must push a clear puff of air (like फ थ ख छ). b d g j zh z must push no air (like प त क च) and are never voiced like ब द ग ज.

Three tongue positions that fix 80% of Indian accents

Flat: z c s

Tongue tip behind the lower front teeth, flat, teeth almost closed. z = त्स (no air), c = त्स + puff, s = स. 在 早 自己 词 四

Curled: zh ch sh r

Tongue tip curled up and back to the roof, like saying ट but held there. zh = च curled, ch = छ curled, sh = ष, r = curled buzz, never trilled. 中 吃 是 日

Smile: j q x

Lips spread in a smile, tongue tip pressed down behind lower teeth, middle of tongue up. j = soft च, q = soft छ, x = between श and स. Always followed by i or ü. 几 七 谢

Vowels and endings 韵母

The part after the consonant. Chinese syllables end cleanly: on a vowel, on n, or on ng. Never add an extra vowel at the end.

Spelling rules that trick everyone:

  • ian sounds like "yen", not "yaan": 点 diǎn = "dyen".
  • iu is short for iou ("yo"), ui for uei ("way"), un for uen ("wun").
  • After j q x y, a written u is really ü: qù, xué, jú, yuè.
  • i after z c s is not "ee": it is just the buzz held (sì = "sz"). After zh ch sh r it is a curled buzz (shì ≈ "shr"). So 是 is never "shee".
  • e alone is a throaty "uh", not ए. ei is "ay". ie and üe are "yeh".
  • -n ends with the tongue tip on the ridge behind your teeth (like न). -ng ends at the back with the tongue tip down (like the ं in अंग). 人 rén vs 能 néng.

Tone changes 变调

Four rules change tones in real speech. Apply them automatically from day 3.

How pinyin is written in this guide: 一 and 不 are shown with their changed tone (because that is what you say). The 3+3 change is not marked; you apply it yourself. So nǐ hǎo is said ní hǎo.

Grammar and culture traps

Grammar in 22 rules 语法

Chinese grammar is simple where Hindi is hard: no verb endings, no gender, no plural endings, no tense conjugation. Learn these 22 patterns and swap nouns into them.

Master trick: build the sentence in Hindi order, then move the verb before the object. मैं (S) कल (time) ऑफिस में (place) कोड (O) लिखूँगा (V) → 我 明天 在办公室 写 代码.

Characters 汉字

You do not need characters to speak. You need them to recognize words on slides, tickets and WeChat or Feishu messages. There are thousands; around 250 cover a big share of everyday office text. Learn these by recognition only: see it, say the sound, know the meaning.

Radicals: the building blocks

Most characters are built from a meaning part (radical) plus a sound hint. Know these 28 and new characters stop looking like random strokes.

Numbers, dates, time 数字和时间

Meetings are full of numbers: deadlines, versions, percentages, counts. Make these instant.

Big numbers via Hindi: Chinese groups by 4 zeros (万 = 10,000), Hindi by lakh and crore, English by thousands. Anchors: 1 lakh = 十万 shí wàn. 10 lakh (1 million) = 一百万 yìbǎi wàn. 1 crore = 一千万 yìqiān wàn. 10 crore (100 million) = 一亿 yí yì.

两 vs 二: counting things uses 两 liǎng (两个人, 两点). Digits, phone numbers, ordinals and versions use 二 èr (第二, 二点零). In phone numbers and room numbers, 1 is often said yāo.

The 15-day plan 十五天计划

Each day builds on the last. Tick a day when you meet its "done when" test, not when you finish reading. Your progress is saved in this browser.

Final checklist (day 15)

Day 16 onward

45 minutes a day keeps it growing: 15 min shadowing, 15 min flashcards, 15 min speaking about your own day. Once a week, repeat the day 14 mock meeting and compare recordings.

Harvest real words: in every Chinese meeting, note 5 words or phrases you heard but didn't know. Look them up after, add them to your own phrase list, say each 10 times.

Ask for tone corrections: tell one friendly colleague 我的声调不对的话，请直接告诉我 wǒ de shēngdiào bú duì de huà, qǐng zhíjiē gàosu wǒ (if my tones are wrong, please tell me directly). This one habit is worth more than any course.

Reactions and fillers 反应和语气词

These make you sound like a participant instead of a spectator. Natives backchannel constantly. Replace your "haan", "okay", "achha", "right right" with these from day 6.

Understanding, agreeing, acknowledging

Fillers: sound natural while you think

Silence while searching for a word sounds like you are lost. A filler sounds like you are thinking. Use 那个 and 怎么说呢 to buy 2 to 3 seconds. Never fill with "umm" or "matlab" in a Chinese sentence.

Meeting phrases 开会用语

Organized by the flow of a meeting. Learn them as a timeline: walk in, update, clarify, discuss, agree, close.

Tech and code talk 技术讨论

Real-world tip: Chinese engineering teams keep many English terms as-is: bug, PR, review, API, commit, Python, Rust, demo, deadline. Say them normally inside Chinese sentences: 这个bug我来修. Learn the Chinese words below anyway, because documents, managers and non-engineers use them.

Sentences for technical discussion

Sentence frames: swap in any noun

Impressions and small talk 给人留下好印象

Chinese workplace culture values humility, acknowledging effort and keeping face. A few of these at the right moment do more for your reputation than perfect grammar.

Small talk and relationship building

Forms of address: senior leaders are often 王总 Wáng zǒng (Director Wang); a respected expert 李老师 Lǐ lǎoshī; a friendly older peer 老王 Lǎo Wáng; a younger peer 小李 Xiǎo Lǐ (only if the team does it). Many tech teams just use English names. Follow what others do. Use 您 with clients and senior leaders, 你 with teammates; 您 with peers sounds stiff.

Face: never say someone is wrong in front of the group. Use 我有一个小建议 or 我们可以再看看, and give the hard feedback one-on-one.

Proverbs 成语和俗语

A well-placed 成语 chéngyǔ (four-character idiom) from a foreigner gets smiles and instant respect. Rule: at most one or two per meeting, and only ones you can say smoothly. Each comes with a ready-made work sentence.

Practice dialogues 对话

Your lines are highlighted. Practice order: read both parts aloud, then play the other person's line and answer from memory, then do it with the text hidden in your head. Replace the details with your real work.

What you will hear 听力

Questions and phrases colleagues say to you, with a ready reply. Train your ear at Native speed once you know them at Clear speed.

Memory system 记忆方法

1. Sound anchors to Hindi

Tie each new sound to a Hindi sound you own: q = छ smiling, zh = च curled, sh = ष. The anchor stops English spelling from hijacking your mouth.

2. Tone colors

Blue sky, green sprout, brown soil, red stop. When you recall a word, recall its color pattern too: 工作 is "blue-red". If you can't picture the colors, you don't know the word yet.

3. Meeting memory palace

Map phrases to places in a meeting room. Door: greetings. Your chair: status update. Whiteboard: technical explanation. The person across: clarify and disagree. Clock: time and deadlines. Exit: closing and thanks. Walk the room in your head and say each station's phrases.

4. Chunks over words

Store 能不能帮我看一下 as one chunk with one melody. In a meeting you retrieve chunks, not grammar. Aim for 300 chunks, not 3000 words.

5. Frames and swaps

Learn a frame once, then generate 10 sentences: 这个___有点问题 with 函数, 接口, 数据, 配置, 方案. Swapping builds speaking speed faster than new vocabulary.

6. Spaced repetition: 1, 3, 7

Review new material the next day, 3 days later, and 7 days later. The flashcards below track what you know. Always review old cards before learning new ones.

7. Self-talk while coding

Narrate your work quietly: 我在写函数… 编译… 报错了… 哎呀… 找到了… 好了. Ten minutes of this a day turns passive words into active speech.

8. Character stories

Break a character into parts and make a tiny absurd story: 忙 = heart 忄 + dying 亡: "so busy my heart is dying". Absurd sticks.

9. Teach it back

At the end of each day, explain the day's rule out loud in Hindi or English to an imaginary junior, with 3 Chinese examples. If you can't teach it, redo it.

What not to do: don't binge 100 new words in a day (they evaporate), don't study only by reading, don't learn words without their tones, and don't wait to "be ready" before speaking to colleagues. Short daily speaking beats long weekend study.

Flashcards 闪卡

Default mode shows English: you say the Chinese out loud before revealing. That trains speaking, not just recognition. Keyboard: Space reveal, 1 again, 2 got it, S speak.

Chinese voice setup 语音设置

Every 🔊 button uses your browser's built-in speech engine, which reads Chinese only if a Chinese (Mandarin, mainland China) voice exists on your device. Set it up once per device, then test it below.

Test your voice

Pass: you hear clear Mandarin with rising and falling tones. Fail: silence, an English voice spelling letters, or a robotic voice ignoring tones. If it fails, follow the steps for your device, then fully close and reopen the browser.

Best choice: Microsoft Edge (Windows, Mac, Android)

Edge includes Microsoft's online natural voices such as Xiaoxiao and Yunxi. They are the most natural free Mandarin voices available in a browser and need no installation, only an internet connection. Open this page in Edge and pick a voice with "Online (Natural)" in its name from the list above.

Windows 11

  1. Open Settings, then Time & language, then Speech.
  2. Under Manage voices, click Add voices.
  3. Search Chinese (Simplified, China), tick it, click Add. This installs Huihui, Yaoyao and Kangkang.
  4. Wait for the download to finish, then close and reopen your browser.

Alternative: Settings, Time & language, Language & region, Add a language, choose 中文(中华人民共和国), and in the optional features keep only Text-to-speech ticked (untick "Set as my display language").

Windows 10

  1. Open Settings, then Time & Language, then Speech.
  2. Under Manage voices, click Add voices and choose Chinese (Simplified, PRC).
  3. If Add voices is missing: Time & Language, Language, Add a language, Chinese (Simplified, China), and install only the Text-to-speech feature.
  4. Restart the browser.

Google Chrome (Windows, Mac, Linux, ChromeOS)

Chrome on desktop ships an online voice named Google 普通话（中国大陆）. It works as soon as you are online, with no install. If it doesn't appear in the list above, update Chrome, then reload the page. When offline, Chrome falls back to your operating system's voices.

Mac (macOS Ventura and newer)

  1. Open System Settings, then Accessibility, then Spoken Content.
  2. Click the System voice menu and choose Manage Voices.
  3. Find Chinese (China mainland) and download Tingting, or an Enhanced or Premium voice for better quality.
  4. You don't need to set it as the system voice. Restart Safari or Chrome.

Older macOS: System Preferences, Accessibility, Speech (or Spoken Content), System Voice, Customize, tick a Chinese (China) voice.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Settings, then Accessibility, then Spoken Content, then Voices.
  2. Choose Chinese (China mainland) and download an Enhanced voice (for example Tingting or Lili).
  3. Turn off Silent mode: iOS mutes browser speech when the phone is silenced.
  4. Reopen Safari. On iOS, audio only starts after you tap, which the 🔊 buttons already require.

Android

  1. Install or update Speech Recognition & Synthesis (also called Speech Services by Google) from the Play Store.
  2. Open Settings and search Text-to-speech (usually under System, Languages & input, or Accessibility).
  3. Set the preferred engine to Speech Services by Google, tap its gear icon, then Install voice data.
  4. Download Chinese (China) / 中文（中国）. Samsung phones: also set the engine from Samsung TTS to Google if Chinese is missing.
  5. Restart Chrome or Edge.

Linux

Use Chrome with its online Google voice (above) for good quality. Offline engines are much weaker: sudo apt install speech-dispatcher espeak-ng gives a working but robotic Mandarin voice in Firefox and Chromium, fine for checking pinyin but not for copying an accent.

ChromeOS

Settings, Accessibility, Text-to-speech, Speech engine, then open Google text-to-speech engine settings and download Chinese. The Chrome online voice also works.

Still no sound?

  • Check device volume and that the tab isn't muted (right-click the tab in desktop browsers).
  • Close every browser window completely after installing a voice. Browsers load the voice list only at startup.
  • Tap any 🔊 button once, then reload. Some browsers load voices lazily.
  • Privacy-hardened browsers and some Firefox configurations disable speech synthesis; try Edge or Chrome.
  • Quality ranking, best first: Edge online natural voices, Apple Enhanced or Premium voices, Google voices in Chrome or Android, Windows offline voices, espeak.
